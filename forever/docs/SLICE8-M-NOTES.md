* `slice: missing action reach cc::{polymorph::cc target::polymorph}`: the mage's approach to a CC mark that is out of spell range. In the one live case the target was in range; in a pack seen from afar `reach cc` has no creator. Register `reach cc` (and check `use trinket`, `current mount speed::self target`).
* Arcane Intellect on the non-mana members loops with BAD_TARGETS (b): a mana filter on the target (G8/H8 decision), not a K8 file.
* G8's `cc <mark> <guid> <bot> [family]` and `cc clear` call `h8::ApplyCcOrder` / `ClearCcOrder` (TbCc.h): both worked live through `.tb cc`. `clear` without mark and unit leaves the group's icon.
