## D-c the late failures (TbOrders.cpp)
* `fail:focus` (3 damage bots, `Run`, verb `focus`): the action `attack rti target` reads the value `rti target` (RtiTargetValue.h `CalculateMark`), which needs the marked unit within SightDistance AND in line of sight; a pack is marked before the pull, usually out of both, so the value is null and the attack is refused. The order IS the mark (group icon + the `rti` value, both set before) and the tank's and damage bots' own target picks read it every tick once the unit is seen. The verb now answers `ok` when the value says "not visible yet" and keeps `fail:focus` for any other refusal (the unit is seen and the attack is still refused).
* `fail:rest` (priest, 12 times in M6): `drink`/`food` refuse when the bot has no item, is above its own "should drink/eat" floors, or in a fight. A rest of a bot with nothing to eat or drink is now a regeneration-only rest: `ok` out of combat (no 30 s sit), `fail:rest` only when the bot or a member near it is in combat or has attackers. In the N6 boot: 0 `focus` and 0 priest `rest` failures; the tank's `rest fail:rest` (6) and `attack fail:attack` (4) are real refusals in a fight.
